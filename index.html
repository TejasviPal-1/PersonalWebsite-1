01 — founding

buildingeve ★ ★ ★

I grew up in Delhi and moved to Canada at seventeen. As I think about my parents getting older, I keep coming back to a question that feels personal to me. How do you show up for someone when you can’t be in the room?

In conversations with family caregivers, I keep hearing about the “default daughter”, the one who remembers the appointments, follows up on results, and keeps her siblings informed. Even when everyone wants to help, she’s often left to work out who does what and make sure it happens. Many millennials are already navigating the sandwich years, raising children while caring for aging parents. As Gen Z approaches that stage of life, I keep wondering how we can help families prepare to share care, with everyone understanding what needs doing and how they can contribute.

That’s why we’re building Eve. I imagine a future where families can understand a loved one’s health, reach the right resources, and find a community that understands what they’re going through. I want Eve to bring those pieces together, using AI to help families make sense of scattered information and take the next step in caring for someone they love, wherever they are. At the heart of that vision is a belief that the person who cares for everyone deserves support, too.
